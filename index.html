About us

Not a general carrier — a specialist.

Founded in 2012, Trans-Jit Express works in one of India's fastest-growing freight segments: alternative fuels and raw materials (AFR) for cement plants.

We pick up from 50+ locations, including Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mangaluru, Coimbatore, Tirupati, Chennai, Kochi, Goa and Haldia, and deliver to 20+ cement plants across Karnataka, Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh.

FAQ

RDF and truck transport: common questions

What is RDF transport?

RDF (refuse-derived fuel) is processed waste that cement plants burn in their kilns in place of coal — this is called co-processing. We transport shredded and unshredded RDF from waste processors to cement plants in high-volume trucks.

Which cement plants do you deliver to?

20+ plants, including ACC Wadi, Chanda and Jamul; UltraTech Rawan, Malkhed, Sedam and Semradih; Ambuja Chandrapur and Bhatapara; Shree Cement Kodla; Chettinad Kallur; Dalmia Belagavi; Heidelberg Damoh and more.

Which areas do you cover?

All of India. Our main lanes run from Maharashtra, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Goa and West Bengal to cement plants in Karnataka, Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh.

What truck types do you have?

12, 14, 16 and 18-wheelers, 20 ft and 32 ft containers, and 40 ft trailers.

Do you carry cargo other than waste?

Yes. We take full truck loads and part loads for any industrial cargo.
